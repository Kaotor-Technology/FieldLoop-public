# Synthetic work procedure: external corrosion review

> SYNTHETIC — Demonstration document. Human review passed.

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how FieldLoop Demo Pipeline Co. (a fictional company) carries out the external corrosion review for DL-01. It covers external metal loss on buried pipe.

## Responsibilities

A qualified reviewer checks the coating survey record and pipe-to-soil potential readings, the work scope and the required controls before work is planned. A second person records the review outcome.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the revision of this procedure.
2. Collect the coating survey record and pipe-to-soil potential readings for Chainage 4,000–5,000 m (SYNTHETIC).
3. Compare what the records show with the approved work scope.
4. Record each finding with its source, for example surface rust at a coating defect on an exposed girth weld.
5. Stop and escalate if the source, location or authorisation is unclear.

## Records

Retain the review record, the coating survey record and pipe-to-soil potential readings and any follow-up request under the document-control process.
