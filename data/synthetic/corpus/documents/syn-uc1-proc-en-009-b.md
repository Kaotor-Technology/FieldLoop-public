# Synthetic work procedure: inspection record quality

> SYNTHETIC — Candidate document for demonstration only. Human review is pending.

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how FieldLoop Demo Pipeline Co. (a fictional company) carries out the inspection record quality for DL-01. It covers the completeness and traceability of inspection records.

## Responsibilities

A qualified reviewer checks a sample of inspection sheets and their source files, the work scope and the required controls before work is planned. A second person records the review outcome.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the revision of this procedure.
2. Collect a sample of inspection sheets and their source files for Chainage 116,000–117,000 m (SYNTHETIC).
3. Compare what the records show with the approved work scope.
4. Record each finding with its source, for example inspection sheets without a recorded inspector or date.
5. Stop and escalate if the source, location or authorisation is unclear.

## Records

Retain the review record, a sample of inspection sheets and their source files and any follow-up request under the document-control process.

## Escalation

If inspection sheets without a recorded inspector or date or a similar finding is recorded, the reviewer notifies the Technical Authority before any work is planned.
