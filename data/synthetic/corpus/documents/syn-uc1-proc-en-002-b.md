# Synthetic work procedure: coating condition review

> SYNTHETIC — Candidate document for demonstration only. Human review is pending.

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how FieldLoop Demo Pipeline Co. (a fictional company) carries out the coating condition review for DL-01. It covers coating adhesion and damage along the Segment.

## Responsibilities

A qualified reviewer checks the coating survey record and dig-site photographs, the work scope and the required controls before work is planned. A second person records the review outcome.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the revision of this procedure.
2. Collect the coating survey record and dig-site photographs for Chainage 18,000–19,000 m (SYNTHETIC).
3. Compare what the records show with the approved work scope.
4. Record each finding with its source, for example disbonded coating at a field joint.
5. Stop and escalate if the source, location or authorisation is unclear.

## Records

Retain the review record, the coating survey record and dig-site photographs and any follow-up request under the document-control process.

## Escalation

If disbonded coating at a field joint or a similar finding is recorded, the reviewer notifies the Technical Authority before any work is planned.
