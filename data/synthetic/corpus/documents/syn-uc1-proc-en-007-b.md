# Synthetic work procedure: valve-station readiness

> SYNTHETIC — Candidate document for demonstration only. Human review is pending.

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how FieldLoop Demo Pipeline Co. (a fictional company) carries out the valve-station readiness for DL-01. It covers block-valve station access, condition and function-test records.

## Responsibilities

A qualified reviewer checks the valve function-test record and the site inspection sheet, the work scope and the required controls before work is planned. A second person records the review outcome.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the revision of this procedure.
2. Collect the valve function-test record and the site inspection sheet for Chainage 88,000–89,000 m (SYNTHETIC).
3. Compare what the records show with the approved work scope.
4. Record each finding with its source, for example an overdue valve function test.
5. Stop and escalate if the source, location or authorisation is unclear.

## Records

Retain the review record, the valve function-test record and the site inspection sheet and any follow-up request under the document-control process.

## Escalation

If an overdue valve function test or a similar finding is recorded, the reviewer notifies the Technical Authority before any work is planned.
